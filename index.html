Many women reach a point where they know they cannot keep leading the same way for much longer. The pressure, the over-functioning, and the constant effort to hold everything together start to feel unsustainable. If you have been putting off your leadership growth, this is your sign to stop waiting.

The Women's Leadership Accelerator is not another training — it is a catalyst for the clarity, confidence, and leadership shift you have been needing.

Judy Ring, MBA

Founder, The Training Ring, LLC

Judy is a seasoned business consultant, executive coach, and keynote speaker with a unique blend of corporate, entrepreneurial, and media experience. She draws on over 20 years in the utility industry — working with national accounts, economic development, conservation programs, service policy, and customer service — as well as 17 years in radio and print media leading high-performing teams.

She specializes in helping individuals and organizations set healthy boundaries, navigate career transitions, build cohesive teams, and achieve sales excellence. Judy brings a sharp strategic mind and a deeply empathetic approach to every engagement.

She holds an MBA, a Bachelor of Applied Science in Energy Management, and an Associate of Applied Science in System Operations, and has served on boards across the country.
